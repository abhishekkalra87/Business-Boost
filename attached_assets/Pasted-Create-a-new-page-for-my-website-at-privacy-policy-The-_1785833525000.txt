# Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 04 August 2026

At NexZenta, we are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use and protect the information you provide through our website, Meta Lead Forms and other communication channels.

## Information We Collect

We may collect:

• Full Name
• Work Email Address
• Phone Number
• Company Name
• Job Title
• Hiring Requirements
• Any information voluntarily submitted through our contact forms.

## How We Use Your Information

Your information is used only to:

• Respond to your enquiries.
• Understand your hiring requirements.
• Provide recruitment and talent acquisition services.
• Contact you regarding our services.
• Improve our customer experience.

## Data Sharing

NexZenta does not sell, rent or trade your personal information.

We may share your information only with trusted partners involved in delivering our recruitment services or where required by law.

## Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, misuse or disclosure.

## Data Retention

We retain your information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected or as required by applicable laws.

## Your Rights

You may request to:

• Access your information
• Correct inaccurate information
• Delete your information
• Withdraw consent for future communications

talent@nexzenta.com

Our website may use analytics and advertising tools to improve user experience. These services may collect anonymous usage information.
